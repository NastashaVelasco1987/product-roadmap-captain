# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First release.

- Four modes: Voyage, Review, Boarding and Meeting soon.
- The seven ports, with the strategic, initiative and feature roadmap templates.
- Every figure comes from the founder with its label. Calculations start with `Calculated:` and show their working.
- Anything without evidence is listed under Gaps to overcome.
- Boundaries for pasted documents, misleading requests, legal questions, confidences, out-of-scope requests and founders in distress.
- Replies in the founder's language. The port names stay in English.
- Installs in Claude Code as a plugin or a personal skill, and in the Claude app by zip upload. Tested in Claude Code.
