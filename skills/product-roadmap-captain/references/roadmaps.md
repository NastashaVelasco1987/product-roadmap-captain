## Which roadmap answers which challenge

| | Strategic | Initiative | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time span it covers | Three to five years | Quarters, up to about eighteen months | Weeks to months |
| Written for | Leadership, the board, investors | The team, pilot customers, partners | Internal only |
| The spine | One business metric, from today to a target | Goals with measurable targets | One goal |
| The rows | Three to five growth levers that visibly reach the target | Initiatives by quarter, each with an owner | Features, each tied to a goal |
| On every row | Value, cost, teams, payback, return | A gate: measure, threshold, decision date, written before the result | Sequence and dependencies |
| What makes it hold | Signals to adapt, runway, a review cadence | The first thirty days specified, one first focus, a stop list | The initiative layer it comes from |

One line each, for Port 2:

- **Strategic:** the whole bet, from today's figure to a target, for the people who fund and govern the company. Usually what an investor means by "show me the roadmap".
- **Initiative:** what the team does by quarter, with a gate on every step, for the people who execute and the partners who join.
- **Feature:** what gets built by month. Useful inside the team; not the document a board, buyer or investor asks for.

Every figure in every template carries a label. Money: signed, forecast, pipeline, market size or target. Operating figures: measured or estimate. A calculated figure starts with `Calculated:` and shows its working.

The time span a roadmap covers is not the horizon for its figures. Value, cost, payback and return always use the one horizon chosen in Port 5: twelve or twenty-four months.

## Strategic roadmap template

```text
THE WHOLE BET
Market opportunity: [ ]
Business arena, what we know or have that others do not: [made explicit]
Product strategy: [how the product wins in that arena]
Commercial model: [how we sell, to whom]
Financial model: [how this becomes sustainable revenue]

BIG STATEMENT
[One sentence a board member could repeat from memory.]

TODAY TO TARGET
Business metric: [one metric, used at both ends]
Today: [figure, may be 0] [label]
Leading metric, if the baseline is zero: [what can move in two quarters]
Target: [figure] [label] by [year]
Horizon for the figures: [12 or 24 months]

INITIATIVES (three to five growth levers, not features)
1. [Name]
   Growth lever: [market / product / channel / platform / partnership]
   Outcome: [what changes in the business]
   Opportunity and how we capture it: [one line]
   Value at the horizon: Calculated: [volume] x [price] x [months live] = [contribution]
   Build cost: [one-off]
   Run cost: [per month, including AI usage, hosting, human review, model upkeep]
   Teams involved: [ ]
   Payback: [months]
   Return: [%] at [horizon]
   Owner: [named role]

THE BRIDGE
Baseline [figure]
+ Initiative 1 [contribution]
+ Initiative 2 [contribution]
= [total] against target [figure]
Gap: [figure, or none]
If there is a gap: [which assumption changes, or which target moves]

TIMELINE
[Year 1]: [live / being tested]
[Year 2]: [ ]
[Year 3]: [ ]
Enablers and compliance first: [ ]

SIGNALS TO ADAPT (thresholds written before any result)
Re-sequence if: [signal, threshold]
Scale if: [signal, threshold]
Drop if: [signal, threshold]
Measured today: [yes / no for each]

RUNWAY AND DEPENDENCIES
Carried on current runway: [yes / no; which initiatives are gated on a raise]
Where investors, customers and partners come in: [ ]

REVIEW
Reviewed: [monthly / quarterly]
Next review: [date]
Stage: [early / in pilots / opportunity validated]
```

## Initiative roadmap template

```text
STRATEGIC AIM THIS SERVES
[ ]

GOALS
1. [goal] - target: [measure and number] - by: [date]

TIME ALLOCATION THIS QUARTER
Innovation: [%]
Data and evaluation: [%]
Trust and compliance: [%]
Maintenance: [%]

SEQUENCE (enablers, data rights and compliance before what depends on them)
| Quarter | Initiative | Outcome | Owner | Evidence of progress | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q[n] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [measure, threshold, date] |

GATES IN FULL
Step: [ ]
Hypothesis it tests: [ ]
Measure: [ ]
Threshold that decides: [written before any result]
Decision date: [ ]
Go: [ ]   Change: [ ]   Stop: [ ]

FIRST INITIATIVE, SPECIFIED FOR 30 DAYS
Initiative: [ ]
Owner: [one named role]
Value it adds to the aim: [ ]
Cost: [time + cash]
Evidence that exists by day 30: [ ]
Decision that evidence informs: [ ]
Week 1: set the baseline
Week 2: build and test
Weeks 3-4: measure with real users
Day 30: decide against the threshold

CAPACITY FIT
Team: [roles, hours a week]
The first focus: [segment, market or product first, and why]

STOP LIST THIS QUARTER (what we will not do)
1.

ON OUR RADAR (known about, not committed to)
1.

MARKET CLOCK
Dates set by someone else: [rule coming into force, partner release, buying cycle, runway]
How the sequence anchors to them: [ ]

REVIEW
Reviewed: [monthly in pilots / every month or two once validated]
Next review: [date]
```
