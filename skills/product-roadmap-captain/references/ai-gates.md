# The Reef: seven AI integrations

Use in Port 4, only when the product depends on work a model does. Ask about one integration at a time: what the output is, and whether the gate is met. A gate not met is not a failure; it becomes an initiative, a risk or a sequence change on the roadmap.

| # | Integration | The question to ask | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI value hypothesis | What outcome changes only because of the model, and by how much? Remove the AI: what does the buyer lose? | A value hypothesis card | At least one buyer would lose something real without the AI. If not, re-scope or reroute |
| 2 | Evaluation spine | What is the error rate on real data, and what does a wrong answer cost? Is there a test set of real examples kept aside from building the model, and a baseline? | An evaluation card and a first measured result | No pilot promise without a measured baseline |
| 3 | Data roadmap | Where will the next labelled real examples come from, and with what rights? | A data plan with a volume and quality target | The next pilot is designed to produce labelled data |
| 4 | Repeatability | What does customer ten cost compared with customer one? Include AI usage and human-review time | Unit-of-work economics | Customer ten costs less than customer one |
| 5 | Trust by design | Which permission or assurance must exist before selling at scale? What happens when it is wrong, and who carries that? | A risk register and a buyer assurance pack | Permissions and assurance are scheduled before scale |
| 6 | Defensibility | If the platform the product runs on ships this next quarter, or the model changes, what does the founder still own? | A dependency map | The founder can say what they own after a competitor or model update |
| 7 | Capacity fit | Who owns each of these, and can the team carry them? | A 90-day plan with owners | Nothing on the roadmap without an owner |

Rules for the Reef:

- An accuracy or performance figure without a kept-aside test set and a baseline is written `[not yet measured]`, and an evaluation initiative is added.
- If there is no model yet, record "no model yet" for integrations 2 to 6 and keep 1 and 7.
- Anything touching personal data, consent or regulation: planning support only, not legal advice. Never say whether something is legal or compliant, and do not quote laws, articles or dates from memory. List the questions for a qualified adviser.
