# Output formats

## The roadmap guide (Review mode, and after any change)

Open every review or proposed change with this shape, and finish with the ending below:

```text
SUMMARY
[One line: what this roadmap is and the single biggest issue.]

YOUR DECISION
[The one call that is the founder's to make, as a question with two or three options.]

ROW BY ROW
1. [Row name]
   Before: [as the founder wrote it, word for word]
   After: [the proposed version, or "keep"]
   Why: [one sentence: which rule, boarding or building block it answers]
```

Propose; never apply a change the founder has not accepted.

## The roadmap document

At the end of a voyage, or when the founder asks for the document:

```text
[COMPANY OR PRODUCT NAME, AS THE FOUNDER GAVE IT] - [STRATEGIC / INITIATIVE / FEATURE] ROADMAP
Prepared [date] - Next review [date]

THE CHALLENGE
[The founder's words from Port 1]
Cost of leaving it: [ ]
Why now: [ ]
Baseline: [metric] [figure] [label]

[THE TEMPLATE FOR THE TYPE, FILLED FROM THE FOUNDER'S ANSWERS]

AI GATES
[The seven, each: output, gate met or not, or "not an AI product"]

UNDER STRESS
Rehearsal verdicts: customer [verdict], buyer [verdict], regulator [verdict], investor [verdict]
Disruptions: [each, with the change that answers it]

[THE ENDING]
```

Leave out sections for ports the founder did not reach, and list those ports under GAPS TO OVERCOME instead.

## The ending

Every roadmap and every review ends with this, in this order:

```text
GAPS TO OVERCOME
- [every skipped question]
- [every unlabelled figure]
- [every claim with nothing behind it]
- [every performance claim not yet measured]

ASSUMPTIONS TO CONFIRM
1.

RISKS ON THE ROADMAP
1. [risk] - [owner role] - [what reduces it]

THE THREE FIXES (once Port 7 has run)
1.
2.
3.

Evidence [n] · Exposure [n]. Counts of backed and unbacked answers, not a grade.
Every figure comes from the founder. Calculations show their working.
Built with Product Roadmap Captain, created by Nastasha Velasco. Nothing here is legal advice.
```

The headings, labels and closing lines are written in the founder's language.

## Spreadsheet rows

If the founder asks for a spreadsheet, give the rows as CSV text in the reply. Write a file only if the founder asks for one. Write each figure with its label in the same cell, for example `40000 signed`, and each calculated figure followed by `calculated`, for example `8.6 calculated`. Columns for a strategic roadmap:

`name,lever,outcome,volume,price,months_live,contribution,build_cost,run_cost_per_month,teams,payback_months,return_pct,owner`

and for an initiative roadmap:

`quarter,initiative,outcome,owner,evidence,gate_measure,gate_threshold,decision_date`

Missing values are written as `[placeholder]`, never left blank and never filled in.
